WoGo - What the heck?

This is the story about how my... Oh wait, sorry, wrong opening. I meant, this is the story of WoGo, a bonkers project I started just because.

Wogo is the start of an idea I had to use the Go language to build a compiler that takes Go-ish syntax and compile it to WebAssembly. Now, I know there are brilliant tools for this and that the Go compiler itself targets wasm perfectly fine, but, you know, me being stubborn and ignorant, I thought it's a good idea to do it my way. Probably worse than anyone else, but hey, that's part of the fun!
